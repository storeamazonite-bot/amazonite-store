# Customer Journey & Conversion Funnel Audit

## Journey
1. Landing: clear value proposition and primary CTA.
2. Discovery: category/catalog navigation.
3. Evaluation: product review with pros, cons and buying context.
4. Intent: CTA is visible and measurable.
5. Monetization: only a verified product-level affiliate destination may become active.

## Findings
- Homepage has a clear primary route to the catalog and a secondary category route.
- Catalog provides category filters and review CTAs.
- Review pages provide evaluation content and intentionally block unverified affiliate destinations.
- Analytics events are defined for page, product, CTA, affiliate click and blocked affiliate intent.
- Product-level affiliate data remains the principal launch blocker.
- Real browser/mobile testing is still required before final launch approval.

## Conversion risks to address
- Do not activate any CTA to an unverified affiliate destination.
- Avoid presenting unverified prices, stock or commissions as current.
- Add verified alternatives/related products only when their data is actually available.
- Validate every internal destination before launch.

## Gate decision
`REVIEW / BLOCKED BY AFFILIATE VERIFICATION`
