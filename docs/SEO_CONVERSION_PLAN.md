# Amazonite Store — SEO & Conversion Plan

## Product page requirements
Each monetized product page should have a unique title, concise value proposition, key verified specifications, price/availability timestamp when available, comparison context where useful, FAQ, clear affiliate disclosure, and one primary CTA.

## Internal linking
- Category -> product
- Buying guide -> product
- Comparison -> competing products
- Product -> related products

## Conversion rules
- Use one clear primary CTA: Check price / View offer.
- Preserve the product ID through the click event.
- Do not claim a discount, stock state, commission, rating or review unless currently verified.
- Open external affiliate destinations safely with `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`.

## Organic growth loop
Refresh pages whose data is stale, improve pages with impressions but weak clicks, and expand content around products with strong verified affiliate economics.
