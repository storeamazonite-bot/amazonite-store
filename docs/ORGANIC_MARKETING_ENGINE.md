# Amazonite Store — Organic Marketing Engine

## Objective
Generate qualified, purchase-intent traffic without paid advertising by turning each verified product into a reusable content and discovery asset.

## Content loop
Product -> buyer intent research -> product page -> SEO article/comparison -> short-form video concept -> social/pin assets -> internal links -> affiliate click -> performance measurement -> optimization.

## Channel roles
- Google Search: high-intent SEO pages, comparisons, buying guides and category pages.
- YouTube: reviews, comparisons, demonstrations and Shorts.
- TikTok / Instagram Reels: short product demonstrations and problem/solution creatives.
- Pinterest: product discovery and evergreen visual content.
- Facebook: useful deal and guide content, with no spam.
- Communities/forums: answer-first participation; affiliate links only where allowed and genuinely useful.

## Product content rule
Do not publish fabricated prices, ratings, reviews, availability, specifications or savings. Time-sensitive facts must be refreshed from an authoritative source.

## One-product content multiplier
For each verified product, create a coordinated set of assets where useful:
1. Product page
2. Search-intent title/description
3. Comparison or buying-guide angle
4. Short video script
5. Social caption
6. Pinterest title/description
7. Internal-link opportunities

## Measurement
Track, where available:
- organic impressions
- visits
- product-page engagement
- affiliate outbound clicks
- conversion/sale signals supplied by the affiliate program
- estimated commission
- content-to-click rate

## Optimization
Prioritize products/content with strong intent, reliable availability, valid affiliate links, acceptable economics and measurable engagement. Retire or refresh stale assets.

## Governance
No automated posting should bypass platform rules, impersonate users, spam communities, or fabricate engagement. Automation is for drafting, scheduling where permitted, monitoring and analysis—not deception.
