Frequently asked questions

Do you sell the products directly?

No. Amazonite Store is an affiliate marketing site. When an eligible product link is active, the purchase is completed through the relevant seller or marketplace.

Can you recommend a product for my situation?

Yes. The best recommendation depends on what you need: use case, budget, features, compatibility and acceptable compromises. We aim to explain those trade-offs clearly.

Are prices and availability guaranteed?

No. Prices, stock, delivery times and seller offers can change. We will avoid presenting temporary information as a permanent guarantee.

Do you use fake customer reviews?

No. We do not invent testimonials, ratings or experiences to create artificial trust.

Which markets does Amazonite target?

Amazonite is designed for shoppers worldwide, with particular commercial focus on the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and European markets.

What happens if I have a problem with an order?

Because the transaction is completed with the seller or marketplace, their order, shipping and refund policies normally govern the purchase. We will clearly direct shoppers to the appropriate seller information rather than pretending to control an order we do not process.
